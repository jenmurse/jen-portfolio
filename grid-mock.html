The idea

I save a lot of outfit inspiration, and my closet never quite catches up to it. The looks I save drift one way and the clothes I buy drift another, and the result is a closet full of things that felt right in the store and never get worn.

How it works

03 What's missing. Across everything you save, the pieces that keep coming back that you don't own, or own too few of.
