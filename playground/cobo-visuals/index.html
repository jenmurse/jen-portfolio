For

Role
Design, direction, and build
Length
2 hours, continuous
Medium
Real-time shaders in Resolume

The brief

Create two hours of visuals for Cobo's DJ set, segmented into five parts that follow the musical arc. Open on space and momentum, then turn more organic to fill the room. Go dark and spacey for a ravey stretch. Transition to a salsa section driven by the hi-hats and bursts of color. End on disco, building to a visual peak that lands with the final musical high before the set winds down.

The sequence

Sixteen moments from the set.

How it was made

I turned each of the five parts into one generative visual that evolves on its own for its whole length. Each track gets its own look, and each big moment lands where Cobo placed it in the set. Every section ends exactly where the next one begins. Each new look grows out of what's already on screen, so the two hours play as one continuous piece.

I designed every section and built it with Claude Code, giving it direction and iterating until a scene felt right. Every frame is drawn live, so nothing repeats, and every clip on this page is rendered straight from the same code.
