The brief

Create two hours of visuals for Cobo's DJ set, segmented into five parts that follow the musical arc. Open on space and momentum, then turn more organic to fill the room. Go dark and spacey for a ravey stretch. Transition to a salsa section driven by the hi-hats and bursts of color. End on disco, building to a visual peak that lands with the final musical high before the set winds down.

The sequence

Sixteen moments from the set, in the order they played. Together they trace the night from deep space to the final disco peak.

On the night

Cobo on the decks with the visuals behind him and everyone grooving on our disco dance floor in front.

How it was made

I turned each of the five parts into one generative visual that evolved on its own for its whole length. Each track got its own look, and each big moment landed where Cobo placed it in the set. Every section ended exactly where the next one began. Each new look grew out of what was already on screen, so the two hours played as one continuous piece.

I designed every section and built it with Claude Code, giving it direction and iterating until a scene felt right. Every frame was drawn live, so nothing repeated, and every clip on this page was rendered straight from the same code.
