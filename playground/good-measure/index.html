Why

Calorie apps are built for packaged food and barcode scanners. If you cook from scratch, there's no good way to see the nutrition of what you actually make, or to plan a week of real meals against your goals. Good Measure is for that, with a shared pantry, recipes with live nutrition, and a weekly plan for each person in the house against their own targets.

How it works

Eight moments from a week of planning, in the order you would use them.

  1. 01The day

    The day at a glance, measured against each person's goals.

  2. 02The week

    A week of meals for each person, with every day's totals underneath.

  3. 03Bring in a recipe

    Paste a recipe's link, and it arrives with its photo and its ingredients matched to the pantry.

  4. 04Fill the pantry

    Add anything the pantry is missing straight from the USDA database, and its nutrition fills itself in.

  5. 05Dial in a recipe

    A recipe, with its nutrition per serving worked out against your goals as you build it.

  6. 06Fix a day

    Pick the targets a day is missing, then choose from three ways to hit them.

  7. 07Keep what worked

    Save a day that worked as a template, and reuse it.

  8. 08Shop the week

    A week of meals becomes one shopping list, grouped by aisle.

Decisions

The pantry is the source of truth. Every ingredient carries its nutrition to the gram, so recipes, days and weeks add up from real food, not estimates.

One kitchen, separate goals. People share a pantry and recipes, but each day is measured against each person's own targets.

Fix a day like for like. The optimizer only swaps a breakfast for a breakfast, so its suggestions are meals you'd actually eat.

Bring your own AI. Good Measure doesn't build a chatbot in. A dedicated MCP connects it to the assistant you already use, like Claude or ChatGPT, which reads your recipes and plans and writes changes straight back. You own the AI; the app is the data.

How it was made

I designed and built it with the help of Claude Code, starting from my own kitchen, where my husband and I cook from the same pantry with different goals.

Good Measure is invite-only, for my friends and family.
