Playground

Experiments and side projects to learn new mediums and tools

Draft intro. I learn a new medium by making a real thing in it, start to finish: an iPhone app I use every day, a planner my household cooks from, two hours of visuals for a room full of people. Claude Code is the main tool, and each project is a test of how far a designer can take an idea alone, from the first sketch to something that ships.
